## 5. VIRGINIA (call 10 AM–1 PM your time) — the two-week-old law

**VSP Central Criminal Records Exchange (CCRE):** (804) 674-6723
(VSP's pages state plainly they cannot give legal advice — keep it to pure logistics and you'll do fine.)
**One circuit court clerk:** Fairfax Circuit Court is the marquee call — it's the ONLY circuit running its own case-management system, so its sealing process differs; get the clerk's criminal/civil division number from fairfaxcounty.gov. Richmond City Circuit is a good second.

Verify — rollout status IS the product here:
- **The headline date:** "Petition sealing under the new law is live as of July 1 — but automatic sealing lists from VSP to the courts start October 1, 2026 — is that right?" (Justice Forward states Oct 1 for the first VSP lists — if CCRE confirms, your VA UI copy gets a precise, sourced sentence no competitor has: *petition now, automatic starts this fall.*)
- "Is the OES sealing petition form now available on the Virginia Judicial System website?" (It was promised for the effective date — confirm it exists and get its name/number.)
- **The no-fee claim:** statute § 19.2-392.12:1(C) says no court fees for automatic-petition sealing; the 2025 amendments reportedly killed fees for regular petition sealing too, though a ~$12 Commonwealth's-Attorney service fee may apply. Ask the circuit clerk: "What does it cost, all-in, to file a sealing petition today?"
- "Is the CCRE online portal for sending criminal history records to circuit courts live yet, or is it still the paper/electronic request process in your two downloadable guides?"
